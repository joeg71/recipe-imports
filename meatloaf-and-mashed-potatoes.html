Meatloaf and Mashed Potatoes

Reconstructed from the video's narration (spoken recipe) — the video's description is a blurb with no recipe. Quantities and steps below are only those actually stated on camera; see "Gaps in this export" for what wasn't specified.

Source: Karissa Stevens – YouTube

Course: Main Dish

Categories: Beef, Potatoe

Collections: Karissa Stevens

Prep: not stated

Cook: ~45 minutes at 350°F for the meatloaf, plus onion and potato prep

Ingredients

  • Semi-caramelized onions (for the meat mixture)

    • 1 onion, thinly sliced

    4 Tbsp salted butter

    ~1/4 tsp salt

    ~1 Tbsp sugar

  • Glaze

    • 1/4 cup dark brown sugar (light brown is fine)

    2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce

    2 Tbsp tomato paste

    1/4–1/2 tsp garlic powder

    1/4 tsp black pepper

  • Meatloaf

    • 2 lb ground beef (she uses 85/15)

    2 eggs

    2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce

    2 Tbsp tomato paste

    1/2 tsp black pepper

    1 tsp garlic powder

    1 tsp salt

    1/4 cup heavy cream

    The semi-caramelized onions, plus all the butter/oil from the pan

    1 cup breadcrumbs

    Bacon, to wrap (optional)

  • Mashed potatoes

    • Russet potatoes, boiled until very tender (quantity not stated)

    Salted butter (start with a couple of Tbsp)

    Salt (start with ~1/2 tsp)

    Black pepper (~1/4 tsp)

    Milk (added ~2 Tbsp at a time to desired consistency)

    Instructions

  • Semi-caramelized onions

    • Preheat a skillet over medium-low heat. Add 4 Tbsp butter and the sliced onion, plus ~1/4 tsp salt and ~1 Tbsp sugar.

    Cook, stirring every few minutes, until softened with a little color — about 10 minutes (not fully caramelized). Keep the butter/oil in the pan with them.

  • Glaze

    • Mix the dark brown sugar, 2 Tbsp Worcestershire, 2 Tbsp tomato paste, garlic powder, and black pepper until combined. (A pastry brush works for both mixing and applying.)

  • Meatloaf

    • Preheat the oven to 350°F.

    In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, eggs, 2 Tbsp Worcestershire, 2 Tbsp tomato paste, black pepper, garlic powder, salt, heavy cream, and the onions with their pan butter/oil.

    Mix the meat and wet ingredients together first, then add the breadcrumbs and mix again (adding breadcrumbs last keeps them from turning into soggy lumps).

    Lay 3 overlapping sheets of plastic wrap on the counter. Arrange bacon strips vertically, side by side.

    Place the meat mixture horizontally across the center of the bacon and shape into a loaf. Use the plastic wrap to roll and press the bacon around the loaf.

    Transfer to a meatloaf pan (or a baking dish). Brush the glaze all over the top and sides.

    Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes.

  • Mashed potatoes

    • Boil the russet potatoes until very tender; drain.

    Add a couple of Tbsp salted butter, ~1/2 tsp salt, and ~1/4 tsp pepper. Start mixing with a hand mixer and let the butter melt.

    Add milk about 2 Tbsp at a time, mixing, until it reaches the consistency you want.

  • Serve

    • Slice the meatloaf and plate with the mashed potatoes.

    Notes

    The onions are deliberately only "semi" caramelized — softened with a bit of color, not cooked down for an hour.

    Bacon is optional but recommended: it renders fat as it cooks and adds flavor. At minimum, make the glaze.

    Mashed potatoes are intentionally plain/classic; a hand mixer gives the best texture.
